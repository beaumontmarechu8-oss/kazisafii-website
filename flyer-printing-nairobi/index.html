What to send for an accurate flyer printing quote

A clear brief helps us understand the job and provide a useful quotation. Before you contact us, gather the details below where possible:

  • The quantity required and the date you need the flyers.
  • Your preferred size, for example A4, A5 or A6, or your own custom dimensions.
  • Whether the flyer should be printed on one side or both sides, and any paper or finish preferences.
  • Your print-ready artwork, or a request to discuss design support before printing.

Not sure about a specification? Send the information you have and ask the team to help you work through the options.

Flyer printing prices, sizes and finishes

Listed base price: KES 20 per flyer · Minimum order: 100 flyers. Matt finish and single-sided printing are included. Gloss adds KES 1 per flyer, Spot UV adds KES 5 per flyer, and double-sided printing adds KES 2 per flyer.

Quantity pricing shown in the live shop: KES 20 each from 1 flyer, KES 18 each from 500, KES 17 each from 1,250, and KES 16 each from 2,500 flyers. The product minimum remains 100 flyers. Standard turnaround is 3 days; express is 2 days (+KES 500), while same-day rush is available in Nairobi (+KES 1,500).

Prices and options are read from the live Kazisafii online shop and may change. Confirm the final product configuration, quantity and quote before checkout.
