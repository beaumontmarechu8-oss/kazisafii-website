What to include when requesting a business card quote

A clear brief helps us understand the job and provide a useful quotation. Before you contact us, gather the details below where possible:

  • The quantity you need and whether the order is for one person or several team members.
  • Your artwork or logo, plus the exact names, job titles, phone numbers, email addresses and website details to appear.
  • Your preferred card size, orientation, paper feel, colour treatment and any finish preferences.
  • Your deadline and whether you need one design or several names using the same layout.

Not sure about a specification? Send the information you have and ask the team to help you work through the options.
