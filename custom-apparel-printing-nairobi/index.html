Choose apparel for your use

Teams may need matching T-shirts or polos, while events and campaigns may call for lighter garments, hoodies, caps or reflective workwear. Start by deciding who will wear the item, when it will be used and whether the order needs several sizes or colours.

Print methods on the T-shirt product

The T-shirt product configurator lists heat press as the included option, with DTF transfer and embroidery available at additional listed charges. These options are product-specific; check the live configurator for the garment and artwork you choose.

Prepare a clear bulk-order brief

For a useful quote, share the garment type, size breakdown, colours, quantities, artwork, names or numbers if needed, and your required date. For a company or school order, include a size list so the team can calculate the quantities accurately.

Artwork and approval

Supply a clear logo or print file and the exact wording. Review spelling, placement and garment colour before confirming the production details. Use our print-ready artwork guide for file preparation tips.

How to request a useful quotation

  1. Tell us the exact product and quantity.
  2. Share finished dimensions, preferred options and intended use.
  3. Attach your logo, photo or artwork, and specify personalisation details where needed.
  4. Include your deadline and delivery destination or collection preference.

Review the artwork, selected options, quantity and final total with the team before confirming production.

Frequently asked questions

Can I order custom apparel for a team or event?

Yes. Share the garment types, size breakdown, colours, quantities, artwork and deadline so the team can confirm the available products and quote.

Which printing methods can I choose?

The T-shirt product configurator lists heat press as included, with DTF transfer and embroidery at additional listed charges. Other garment options may have different methods, so check the selected product.

How do I prepare a bulk apparel order?

Prepare a size and quantity list for each garment, supply the artwork and brand colours, and confirm the placement and deadline before production.
