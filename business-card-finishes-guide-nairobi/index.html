Which finish should you choose?

Matt for a clean, understated look

Matt is included in the listed base price. It suits a restrained, professional layout where you want the card to feel simple and easy to read.

Gloss for brighter-looking colour

Gloss adds a reflective surface and is listed at an additional KES 1 per card. It can suit colourful designs and image-led branding.

Spot UV to highlight a detail

Spot UV adds a gloss treatment to chosen areas and is listed at an additional KES 5 per card. It is useful when you want a logo or selected design element to stand out.

Double-sided for more information

Double-sided printing is listed at an additional KES 2 per card. It gives you another side for a QR code, services, social links or a secondary language.

What information belongs on a business card?

Keep the card focused on the details a customer needs to contact you. Depending on the role and brand, this may include:

  • Your name and job title or business role.
  • Business or brand name and a clear logo.
  • Phone number and professional email address.
  • Website or one useful social profile.
  • A QR code only when it opens a relevant page and is tested before print.

For a team order, prepare one consistent layout and a checked list of each person's name, title and contact details. This reduces last-minute corrections and makes the set look consistent.

Artwork tips before you order

  • Use a high-resolution logo or vector file where possible.
  • Make sure phone numbers and email addresses are legible at the final card size.
  • Keep important wording away from the trim edge and follow the print template's safe area.
  • Check spelling, punctuation, QR destinations and every staff member's details.
  • Export a print-ready PDF and keep the editable source file for later updates.
