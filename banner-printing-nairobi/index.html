What to include when requesting a banner quote

A clear brief helps us understand the job and provide a useful quotation. Before you contact us, gather the details below where possible:

  • The finished width and height, or the space available for the banner.
  • Where it will be used: indoors, outdoors, at a shop, on a stand or at an event.
  • The quantity, preferred orientation and any mounting or finishing requirements.
  • Your artwork or logo, the message to print and the date you need the banner.

Not sure about a specification? Send the information you have and ask the team to help you work through the options.

Frequently asked questions

What dimensions should I choose for a banner?

Measure the available display space and consider how far away viewers will stand. Send those dimensions and a photo of the location if possible so the team can advise on the brief.

Can I request a banner for an event?

Yes. Include the event date, venue, display area, dimensions and quantity so the team can discuss the suitable option.

Do you install banners?

Installation depends on the banner type and site requirements. Include your location and mounting needs when requesting a quote so the team can confirm what is available.
