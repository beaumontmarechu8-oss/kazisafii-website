Current listed pricing and options

The listed base price is KES 1,000 per T-shirt, with a minimum order of 1 piece.

Available sizes shown in the product configurator are S, M, L, XL and XXL. Colours include white, black, red, maroon, navy, cobalt, sky blue, green, yellow, orange, pink, grey and a custom-colour option. Heat press is included; DTF transfer adds KES 50 and embroidery adds KES 250.

Bulk pricing: KES 1,000 each from 1; KES 900 each from 10 (10% off); KES 850 each from 25 (15% off); and KES 800 each from 50 (20% off).

Prices are the listed base prices in Kazisafii’s live shop and may change when product options, print method, artwork or quantity are changed. Confirm the final configuration and total before checkout.

What to include when ordering

  • Your quantities by size (S to XXL) and preferred colour.
  • Your logo or artwork, or the wording, names and numbers you want printed.
  • Your preferred print method: heat press, DTF transfer or embroidery.
  • Your deadline and whether the order is for one item or a group.

Use the online product page to choose available options, upload your artwork or open the design studio. You can also ask the team for help through WhatsApp.

Turnaround and delivery

Kazisafii’s shop lists standard production at 3 days, express at 2 days for an additional KES 500, and same-day rush for Nairobi orders for an additional KES 1,500. Confirm the availability of the selected turnaround for your chosen product before placing the order.

Collection is free. Delivery is listed at KES 400 within Nairobi and KES 850 elsewhere in Kenya. Delivery is free for orders over KES 10,000.

Frequently asked questions

How much does T-shirt printing cost in Nairobi?

The listed base price is KES 1,000 per T-shirt. Quantity discounts start at 10 pieces, with lower listed unit prices at 25 and 50 pieces. DTF transfer and embroidery have additional charges.

Can I order just one custom T-shirt?

The live product configurator lists a minimum quantity of one T-shirt. You can choose size, colour and print method before confirming the order.

Can I add names or numbers?

The product configurator includes an optional field for names and numbers. Include the exact spelling and placement instructions when submitting the design.
