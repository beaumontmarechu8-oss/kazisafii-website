Select a format for your message

Flyers and leaflets work for short announcements and offers; brochures give space to explain several products or services; menus need clear sections and pricing; posters are suited to notices and displays. Choose the format based on how much information you need to communicate.

Make the message easy to scan

Lead with the most important information, keep contact details readable and use a clear call to action. For a menu, double-check product names and prices. For event materials, verify dates, venue, schedule and registration details.

Supply the details for your quote

Share the finished size, quantity, paper or finish preferences, whether printing is one- or two-sided, your artwork and deadline. Minimum quantities in the table are the catalogue values where listed; confirm the current product configurator before checkout.

Prepare artwork and approve the proof

Use print-ready artwork and check all text carefully before production. For help with margins, images and PDF export, read the print-ready artwork guide.

How to request a useful quotation

  1. Tell us the exact product and quantity.
  2. Share finished dimensions, preferred options and intended use.
  3. Attach your logo, photo or artwork, and specify personalisation details where needed.
  4. Include your deadline and delivery destination or collection preference.

Review the artwork, selected options, quantity and final total with the team before confirming production.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a flyer and a brochure?

A flyer is usually a compact single-sheet promotion, while a brochure gives more space to explain products, services or information across panels or pages. Choose based on the amount of content you need.

What is the minimum order for flyers?

The catalogue lists flyers from KES 20 each with a minimum quantity of 100. Confirm the live product configurator for current options and pricing.

Can I print menus or event programmes?

The catalogue includes menus and funeral programmes. Share the format, finished size, quantity, artwork and deadline when requesting a quote.
