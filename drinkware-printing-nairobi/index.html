Choose drinkware for its purpose

Ceramic mugs suit desk use, enamel mugs give a different look, travel mugs are designed for drinks on the move, and water bottles make practical gifts or branded merchandise. The right choice depends on how the item will be used and the kind of personalisation you need.

Prepare artwork that works on the item

Send a clear logo or design and tell us where it should appear. For brand marks, use a high-resolution file or vector artwork where available. Check spelling, colours and the final layout before approving the proof.

What to include in your quote request

Share the item, quantity, print area or personalisation, artwork, preferred colours and deadline. For company or event orders, mention whether the items need individual names or one consistent design.

How to request a useful quotation

  1. Tell us the exact product and quantity.
  2. Share finished dimensions, preferred material or options and intended use.
  3. Attach your logo, image or print-ready artwork, or tell us what design help you need.
  4. Include your deadline and delivery destination or collection preference.

Review the specifications, artwork and final total with the team before confirming production.
