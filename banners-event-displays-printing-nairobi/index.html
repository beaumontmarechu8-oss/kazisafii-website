Choose the type of display

A standard banner may suit a shop promotion or temporary display. Roll-up and X banners are portable stand options. Flags and teardrop displays work for outdoor or event visibility, while backdrops and pop-up displays provide a larger branded area. Confirm the exact stand, size and included hardware for each product.

Plan around the venue

Before ordering, measure the available space and consider how far away people will read the message. Share whether the display is indoor or outdoor, how it will be mounted, and whether it needs to be moved between venues.

Prepare readable artwork

Use a concise headline, a clear logo and only the information the viewer needs. Make contact details legible at the intended viewing distance. For large artwork, confirm the final dimensions and file requirements before exporting.

Quote and production details

Send the product type, dimensions, quantity, artwork, venue and required date. Check the selected product’s listed base price and finish options in the online shop and confirm the final total before production.

How to request a useful quotation

  1. Tell us the exact product and quantity.
  2. Share finished dimensions, preferred options and intended use.
  3. Attach your logo, photo or artwork, and specify personalisation details where needed.
  4. Include your deadline and delivery destination or collection preference.

Review the artwork, selected options, quantity and final total with the team before confirming production.
