Choose the format for the image

Small photo prints are useful for albums and sharing; mounted, framed, canvas, acrylic and wooden prints create display pieces; puzzles, calendars, cushions and mugs turn photos into gift items. Decide where the item will be displayed or used before selecting the format.

Use a suitable original image

A high-quality original photograph generally gives a better result than a screenshot or a compressed social-media image. Check that the image is sharp, correctly oriented and has enough resolution for the final size.

Personalisation details

For a gift item, provide the exact photo, name, date or message you want included. Confirm the available personalisation area and preview the layout before you approve the order.

Order and delivery

Share the product, finished size where applicable, quantity, image file and deadline. Prices and options may change with product variants; confirm the current product selection and final amount at checkout.

How to request a useful quotation

  1. Tell us the exact product and quantity.
  2. Share finished dimensions, preferred options and intended use.
  3. Attach your logo, photo or artwork, and specify personalisation details where needed.
  4. Include your deadline and delivery destination or collection preference.

Review the artwork, selected options, quantity and final total with the team before confirming production.
