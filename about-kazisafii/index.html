How ordering works

1. Choose a product

Browse the shop and open the product page to review its available options, starting price and minimum quantity.

2. Prepare your design

Use the online design studio where available, upload your artwork, or contact the team with your brief and logo.

3. Confirm the details

Check the quantity, options, artwork, total and required date with the team before production begins.

4. Pay and follow your order

The website supports M-Pesa payments and includes order tracking. Follow the instructions shown during checkout and in your order confirmation.

Serving Nairobi and customers across Kenya

Kazisafii is based in Nairobi, Kenya. The shop advertises same-day printing availability in Nairobi for eligible orders and delivery options beyond Nairobi. Timing and delivery charges depend on the product, quantity, destination and production requirements; check the current details at checkout or confirm them with the team.

Orders above KES 10,000 qualify for free delivery according to the shop's published offer. For urgent work, confirm availability before placing your order.

Questions about a product or order?

Share the product name, quantity, artwork or personalisation details, and the date you need it. The team can help confirm the options and next steps.
