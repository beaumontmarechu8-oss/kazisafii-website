What to include when requesting a mug printing quote

A clear brief helps us understand the job and provide a useful quotation. Before you contact us, gather the details below where possible:

  • The number of mugs you need and whether each item has the same design or individual names.
  • Your logo, photo or message, plus any brand colours or layout preferences.
  • The intended use: personal gift, corporate merchandise, event keepsake or promotional item.
  • Your required date and any packaging or presentation preferences.

Not sure about a specification? Send the information you have and ask the team to help you work through the options.

Custom mug printing prices and options

Listed base price: KES 420 per mug · Minimum order: 1 mug. Standard printing is included; full-wrap printing adds KES 60 per mug. Available colours shown in the shop include white, black, red, maroon, navy, cobalt, sky blue, green, yellow, orange, pink, grey and custom colour.

Quantity pricing shown in the live shop: KES 420 each from 1, KES 378 each from 10, KES 357 each from 25, and KES 336 each from 50 mugs. Standard turnaround is 3 days; express is 2 days (+KES 500), while same-day rush is available in Nairobi (+KES 1,500).

Prices and options are read from the live Kazisafii online shop and may change. Confirm the final product configuration, quantity and quote before checkout.
