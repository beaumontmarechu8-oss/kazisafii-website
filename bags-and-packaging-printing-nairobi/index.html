Choose packaging for the product

Think about the size and weight of the item being packed, how the bag or box will be carried, and whether it is for retail, delivery, an event or a gift. Share approximate dimensions and intended use when asking for a quote.

Bags for shops, events and promotions

Tote, jute, kraft, paper shopping and drawstring bags can suit different retail, gifting and event needs. Select the style first, then confirm the available colours, material and branding method for the exact product.

Boxes and presentation packaging

Packaging boxes, mailer boxes, gift boxes and presentation folders can help organise or present products. Confirm the item size, internal fit, quantity and whether the packaging will be shipped, handed over or displayed.

Prepare your brand artwork

Send your logo in a clear file, specify the placement you want and include brand colours where consistency matters. Ask the team to confirm print area and available options for the selected bag or box.

How to request a useful quotation

  1. Tell us the exact product and quantity.
  2. Share finished dimensions, preferred options and intended use.
  3. Attach your logo, photo or artwork, and specify personalisation details where needed.
  4. Include your deadline and delivery destination or collection preference.

Review the artwork, selected options, quantity and final total with the team before confirming production.

Frequently asked questions

Can bags or packaging be branded with my logo?

Share your logo and the exact bag or box you are considering. The team can confirm the available branding area and options for that product.

Do all bags and boxes have the same minimum quantity?

No. Minimums vary by product. The catalogue lists minimums for selected items such as kraft bags, paper shopping bags and packaging boxes; confirm the live product configurator for the item you choose.

What details should I include in a packaging quote?

Include the product, dimensions, quantity, intended use, artwork and required date. For boxes, share the approximate item size that needs to fit inside.
