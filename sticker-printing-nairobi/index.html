Custom sticker printing for your requirements

Stickers and labels can identify products, carry brand details, seal packaging or support a promotion. The best specification depends on the surface, size, shape and environment where the sticker will be used, so include those details when asking for a quote.

Details that help us quote custom stickers

A clear brief helps us understand the job and provide a useful quotation. Before you contact us, gather the details below where possible:

  • The sticker or label dimensions, shape and approximate quantity.
  • The surface it will be applied to, such as packaging, glass, plastic or another material.
  • Whether the stickers will be used indoors or outdoors, and whether they need to resist handling or moisture.
  • Your artwork, preferred colours and whether you have a sample or reference to share.

Not sure about a specification? Send the information you have and ask the team to help you work through the options.

Frequently asked questions

Can I request stickers for product packaging?

Yes. Share the product type, packaging surface, sticker size, shape and quantity. Include a photo of the packaging if it helps explain the application.

Can stickers be made in a custom shape?

Share the shape and dimensions you need. The team can confirm the available production options and quote accordingly.

What artwork should I send?

A high-resolution or print-ready file is helpful. Send the available file and the team can advise whether any preparation is needed.
