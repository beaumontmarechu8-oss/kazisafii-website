How is pricing confirmed?

Every order is quoted against the exact quantity, size, material, print method, finishing and artwork requirements. Our team confirms your final price before production begins.

Can you help with design?

Yes. Tell us whether you already have print-ready artwork or need help creating or preparing a design. We’ll confirm the available options and any design charges before proceeding.

Do you deliver outside Nairobi?

Delivery arrangements can be discussed when you request a quote. We’ll confirm the delivery cost and timing for your location.

How do I pay?

Payment details are shared when your quote and order are confirmed. M-Pesa is accepted; please wait for our team to confirm the correct payment instructions before sending money.

How do I check an order?

Contact our team on WhatsApp with your order details and we’ll help you with an update.
