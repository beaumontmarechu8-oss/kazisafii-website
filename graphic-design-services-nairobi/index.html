Design support for business and print projects

A clear design helps people recognise your business and understand your message. The catalogue lists design options for logos, business cards, flyers and social media graphics. Describe what you need and how the design will be used so the scope can be agreed first.

Prepare a useful design brief

Share your business name, audience, purpose, preferred wording, brand colours, examples you like and any existing logo or images. For print work, include the finished size, quantity and where the design will be printed.

Artwork checks before printing

Before approval, check names, phone numbers, dates, spelling, dimensions and visual balance. For print-ready files, confirm bleed, safe margins, image resolution and colour requirements for the selected print method.

How to request a useful quotation

  1. Tell us the exact product and quantity.
  2. Share finished dimensions, preferred material or options and intended use.
  3. Attach your logo, image or print-ready artwork, or tell us what design help you need.
  4. Include your deadline and delivery destination or collection preference.

Review the specifications, artwork and final total with the team before confirming production.
