Select the right printed format

Journals and notebooks work for everyday notes, training or branded stationery. Exercise books are suited to school and learning use. Booklets and magazines allow multi-page content, while books and photo books support longer-form or image-led projects.

Plan the pages before requesting a quote

Prepare the page count, finished size, orientation, cover details and interior artwork. For multi-page work, make sure pages are in the correct order and that images and text remain within safe margins.

What to share with the print team

Tell us the product type, final size, page count, quantity, cover and paper preferences, finishing, artwork status and deadline. For receipt or invoice books, confirm the number of copies or sets and any numbering or duplicate requirements.

How to request a useful quotation

  1. Tell us the exact product and quantity.
  2. Share finished dimensions, preferred material or options and intended use.
  3. Attach your logo, image or print-ready artwork, or tell us what design help you need.
  4. Include your deadline and delivery destination or collection preference.

Review the specifications, artwork and final total with the team before confirming production.

Frequently asked questions

Can you print journals and exercise books?

Both journals and notebooks and exercise books are listed in the catalogue. Share the size, quantity and any branding requirements for a quote.

Can I print a booklet or magazine?

Booklets and magazines are available in the catalogue. The quote depends on page count, finished size, paper, cover, quantity and finishing.

What artwork should I send for a book?

Send a print-ready PDF when available, with the pages in order and the cover supplied separately if requested. If you need design or layout help, discuss it with the team before production.
